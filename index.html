MARINA ALCÁZAR CID

Doctoral Researcher in Labour Economics

Hi, I'm Marina, welcome!

I'm a PhD candidate in Economics at Paris School of Economics (PSE), with a scholarship from Fundación Ramón Areces, and working under the supervision of Andrew Clark and Judit Vall Castelló.

I'm working at the intersection between Labour, Gender and Family Economics. My research focuses on how labour market dynamics shape gender inequality and family formation.

Previously, I worked as Research Assistant at the DG Economics of the European Central Bank and the Bank of Spain, where I worked on labour markets and household finances.

Beyond academia, I work to promote European civic engagement and dialogue. I'm Research Lead and Co-founder of the Wealth Cap Project and President and Co-founder of Club Alpbach Spain.
