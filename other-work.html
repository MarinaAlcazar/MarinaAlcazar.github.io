The Wealth Cap Project (tWCP)

I am Co-founder and Research Lead of the Wealth Cap Project, a research initiative examining the implications of wealth concentration for economic stability, social cohesion, and democratic governance. The project bridges academic research and public deliberation, notably through citizens' assemblies that bring debates on extreme wealth into the public sphere. I help shape and implement the project's research agenda to inform evidence-based policy discussions on wealth inequality.

  • Research: Collaborating with the WEIPO team alongside Olga Cantó and Francisco García-Rodríguez at Universidad de Alcalá on wealth taxation and its fiscal implications in Spain.
  • Citizens' Assemblies:
    • Co-chaired, alongside Nhat An Trinh, Your Voice on Wealth: A Citizens' Assembly at the European Forum Alpbach in August 2026, the first European youth citizens' assembly focused on limiting wealth concentration and inequality.
      Read the final report here!
    • Collaborating with IPP-CSIC Laboratory to organize a citizens' assembly on wealth inequality and taxation in Spain.
  • Public Engagement and Advocacy: Moderated sessions and contributed to public discussions on wealth inequality and taxation, including:
    • Is Inequality Europe's Hidden Vulnerability, at the European Forum Alpbach 2026.
    • Jornada the Wealth Cap Project, at the Spanish Congress of Deputies in September 2026.

Club Alpbach Spain

I am Co-founder and President of Club Alpbach Spain, a non-profit organization fostering European civic engagement and facilitating dialogue and collaboration among young professionals and students.

  • Events and Dialogue: Lead the organization of events, workshops, and discussions on European and global affairs in collaboration with the team.
  • Scholarships and Access: Help coordinate scholarships enabling Spanish students and young professionals to participate in the European Forum Alpbach.
  • Delegation Coordination: Accompanied and supported the Spanish delegation at the European Forum Alpbach 2026.
