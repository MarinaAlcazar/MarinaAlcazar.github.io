Volunteer Work

The Wealth Cap Project (tWCP)

I'm Research Lead and Co-founder of the Wealth Cap Project, a research initiative exploring the implications of wealth concentration for economic stability, social cohesion, and democratic governance. A central pillar of tWCP is the organization of citizens' assemblies that bring academic debates on extreme wealth into the public sphere. I contribute to the design and implementation of the project's research agenda to inform policy discussions on wealth inequality.
  • I'm currently collaborating with the WEIPO team at Universidad de Alcalá on a report simulating the tax revenue effects of different wealth cap scenarios in Spain.
  • I'll also be co-chairing, together with Nhat An Trinh, the first European youth citizens' assembly on wealth concentration and inequality, "Your Voice on Wealth: A Citizens' Assembly", at the European Forum Alpbach in August 2026.

Club Alpbach Spain

I'm President and Co-founder of Club Alpbach Spain, a non-profit organization promoting European civic engagement, dialogue, and collaboration among young professionals and students.
  • Together with the rest of the team, I organize events, workshops, and discussions on European and global affairs.
  • I also help coordinate scholarships that enable Spanish students and young professionals to attend the European Forum Alpbach.
  • I'll be accompanying this years Spanish delegation to the European Forum Alpbach and moderating the session "Is Inequality Europe's Hidden Vulnerability"!
